# Waypoint 12.1.0 — Toolbox Edition

Adds a unified in-trip Toolbox across Web, iOS and Android.

## Included tools
- Live currency converter using Waypoint's FX endpoint.
- Tip calculator.
- Unit converter: distance, weight, temperature and liquid volume.
- Destination-time lookup using IANA time zones.
- Fuel-cost estimator for MPG or L/100km workflows.
- Luggage-weight checker.
- Equal split-bill calculator.
- Departure-time / ETA helper with travel time and safety buffer.
- AI quick tools for day planning, optimization, rain plans, budget plans, packing, conflicts, romantic plans and full-trip planning.
- Quick links to Emergency, Packing, Wallet, Tasks, Polls, Search, Flights and Recap.

Premium gates remain in place for advanced AI/full itinerary features.
