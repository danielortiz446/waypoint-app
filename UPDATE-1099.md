# Waypoint V10.9.9

- Receipt images: accepts JPG/PNG/WebP selections up to 12 MB; large images are optimized client-side before OCR and the server accepts up to 5 MB processed payload.
- Smart packing: fixed blank-item bug (`name` vs `text`), added visible suggestion preview, weather/trip-type reasons, and deduplication.
- Privacy/Terms: bilingual EN/ES switch is available on legal pages and now also on the first-use legal gate. Legal pages are bundled into iOS/Android web assets.
- AI: complete itineraries/full trip plans are Premium-only in the UI and server. Free users retain idea/recommendation queries subject to daily limits.
- Version/cache bumped to 10.9.9 across web/iOS/Android.
